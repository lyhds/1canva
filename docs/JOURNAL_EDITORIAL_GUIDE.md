# Journal Editorial and Cover Guide

The Journal supports purchase decisions and should strengthen trust in the
store's hand-painted offering. Do not present generated studio scenes, hands,
artists, materials, or production steps as documentary evidence.

## Editorial rules

- Prefer useful, durable subjects: sizing, placement, colour, light, texture,
  framing, and choosing a subject for a room.
- Keep production claims factual and verifiable. Avoid fixed materials,
  timelines, layer counts, or “not a mock-up” claims unless they are confirmed
  for every relevant product.
- Use the first article tag as the homepage category label.
- Write a concise summary that makes sense without the article title.
- Add descriptive alt text to every cover image.

## Cover-image rules

Use sources in this order:

1. A genuine close-up or room photograph of the store's product.
2. A licensed real photograph from a source such as Unsplash.
3. Open-access or public-domain artwork from a museum collection.
4. A clearly decorative generated texture, never a simulated documentary
   photograph. Generated imagery should not be used when it could imply proof
   of materials, authorship, or production.

When using Unsplash, select a free Unsplash License image rather than an
Unsplash+ image. Keep the source page and photographer credit in the article.
